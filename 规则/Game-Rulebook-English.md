COMPLETE ENGLISH RULEBOOK

2–4 Players | 15–30 Minutes

Monsters assemble—seize your territory!

# 1. Game Overview
Xiyao: Seize Territory is a light party card game inspired by the monsters of Journey to the West. Recruit Monsters, occupy Territories, reinforce your Defenders, and attack other players.
# 2. Winning the Game
Immediately win when you control all 3 Territories in the same Domain. The four Domains are White Bone, Fire Cloud, Lion-Camel, and Spider Silk.

# 4. Play Areas and Key Terms
- Shared Deck: The combined Monster and Treasure deck used by all players.
- Territory Deck: A separate deck containing the 12 Territory cards.
- Central Territory Area: The face-up area for unclaimed Territories. Keep 3 face-up whenever possible.
- Hand: Cards held but not yet played. The end-of-turn hand limit is 7.
- Discard Pile: Discarded cards, defeated Monsters, and resolved Treasures are placed here.
- Defender: A Monster currently stationed in a Territory.
- Origin Territory: Your Territory from which attackers are sent.
- Target Territory: Another player’s Territory being attacked.
# 5. Setup
1. Shuffle all Monster and Treasure cards together to form the Shared Deck.
1. Each player draws 5 cards as their starting hand.
1. Shuffle the 12 Territory cards to form the Territory Deck.
1. Reveal 3 Territories in the Central Territory Area.
1. Randomly choose the first player.
1. Take turns clockwise.
No player controls a Territory at the start of the game.
# 6. Central Territories and Control
Keep 3 unclaimed Territories face-up whenever possible. Claim an unclaimed Territory only through Recruit; it cannot be targeted by Seize Territory. Refill the Central Territory Area after a Territory is claimed.
A Territory is controlled by the player whose Defender is stationed there. Each Territory can hold up to 3 Defenders, all belonging to the same player.
# 7. Turn Structure
1. Draw Phase: Draw 1 card.
1. Main Action Phase: Take 1 Main Action or pass.
1. Resolution Phase: Fully resolve the action and all resulting effects.
1. End Phase: If you have more than 7 cards, discard down to 7.
1. Check the winning condition and empty-deck condition.
If the Shared Deck is empty, skip the Draw Phase. Extra actions and reactions granted by cards do not count against the normal limit of 1 Main Action.
# 8. Main Actions
- Recruit
- Seize Territory
- Use a Treasure
Before starting an action, confirm that it has a legal target or can legally resolve.
# 9. Recruit
Play 1 Monster from your hand into either an unclaimed Central Territory or a Territory you control with fewer than 3 Defenders. It becomes a Defender. Resolve its entry effect after it legally enters play. A Monster in hand cannot directly join a Seize Territory attack.
# 10. Seize Territory
1. Choose one of your Territories containing at least 1 Defender as the Origin Territory.
1. Choose another player’s Territory containing at least 1 Defender as the Target Territory.
1. Choose 1–3 Defenders from the Origin Territory as attackers.
1. Unchosen Defenders remain behind.
1. Resolve the attackers one at a time using the battle rules.
You cannot attack your own Territory, an unclaimed Territory, or a Territory protected from attack.
# 11. Stars, Health, and Damage
A Monster’s Star value is both its Health and Damage. A Monster is defeated when accumulated Damage equals or exceeds its Stars. All surviving Monsters fully recover after battle. Treasures have no Stars and do not fight.
# 12. Battle Sequence
1. The attacking player chooses the order of attackers.
1. Each attacker attacks at most once.
1. Choose 1 surviving Defender as the target.
1. The attacker deals Damage equal to its Stars. The Defender simultaneously counterattacks for Damage equal to its Stars.
1. Damage is simultaneous.
1. A Monster whose accumulated Damage equals or exceeds its Stars is defeated.
1. Damage on defending Monsters accumulates throughout the battle.
1. If no Defenders remain, stop and resolve success. Otherwise continue with the next attacker.
# 13. Battle Results
## Success
No Defender survives and at least 1 attacker survives. Move all surviving attackers into the Target Territory; the attacking player takes control.
## Failure
After all attackers act, at least 1 Defender remains. Surviving attackers return to the Origin Territory and control does not change.
If both sides are defeated, the Target Territory returns to the Territory Deck. An empty Origin Territory also returns immediately.
# 14. Returning Empty Territories
Whenever a controlled Territory loses its last Defender, return it to the Territory Deck and shuffle. Refill the Central Territory Area until 3 Territories are face-up or the deck is empty.
# 15. Treasures and Reactions
Using a Treasure normally takes your Main Action. Play it, choose legal targets, resolve its effect, then discard it. Ongoing Treasures follow their individual rules.
Diamond Ring is a reaction: when another player plays a Treasure, you may immediately play Diamond Ring to cancel that effect. This does not use your Main Action.
# 16. Common Card Terms
- Return to hand: Move a Defender to its owner’s hand. This is not defeat and does not trigger “when defeated” effects.
- Defeat: Put the Defender into the discard pile unless it has a replacement effect.
- Put into your hand: Move the chosen card into the effect user’s hand and change ownership.
- Place in your Territory: Move the Defender into a legal Territory you control with an open slot.
- Randomly: Determine the result from all legal choices using an agreed random method.
- Secretly choose: Choose without revealing until all selections are locked.
- Protect: A protected object cannot be targeted in the prohibited way.
# 17. Card Resolution Rules
- Resolve card effects exactly as written.
- Reduce quantities or choose alternatives only when the card allows it.
- An effect without a legal target does not resolve.
- Costs are paid first and are normally not refunded.
- Entry effects resolve only after legal entry.
- Resolve Territory return immediately when the last Defender leaves.
# 18. Hand Limit
Check the hand limit only during the active player’s End Phase. If that player has more than 7 cards, they choose and discard down to 7. Other players wait until the end of their own turns.
# 19. When the Shared Deck Is Empty
Do not reshuffle the discard pile. Continue playing without drawing.
If a complete round passes with no Monster or Treasure played and no Territory changing control, compare:
1. Number of controlled Territories.
1. Total Stars of your Defenders.
1. Most Territories controlled in a single Domain.
1. Number of Defenders in play.
1. If still tied, the game is a draw.
# 20. Battle Examples
## 1-Star attacks 1-Star
Each deals 1 Damage and both are defeated.
## 2-Star attacks 1-Star
The 2-Star defeats the 1-Star, survives 1 counterattack Damage, and fully recovers after battle.
## Two 1-Stars attack a 5-Star
The two attackers deal 2 total Damage and are both defeated. The 5-Star survives, so the attack fails.
## Three 4-Stars attack a 5-Star
The first 4-Star deals 4 Damage and is defeated. The second defeats the 5-Star through accumulated Damage. At least one attacker survives, so the attack succeeds.
